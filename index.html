Forward-looking portfolio — market-implied to Dec 31, 2026

Same three views as the backtest, but forward: what the market implies for your portfolio at Dec 31, 2026, from the S&P 500 close bands. The S&P distribution is direct market-implied data; each ETF is a proxy mapped to it through its beta to VOO (no historical alpha assumed), and the portfolio distribution comes from 4,000 simulated paths that keep the correlations between ETFs. Everything here recalculates when you change weights. Nothing is projected past Dec 31 — no market prices a longer horizon.

Method and notes

Rebalanced monthly to your target weights: each month's portfolio return is the weighted average of the ETFs' monthly returns. The starting point is the first month's adjusted close in the selected range, compounded monthly from there.

Annualized volatility is the sample standard deviation of monthly returns multiplied by √12. Annualized return is the compound annual growth rate over the selected period.

Drawdown recovery: for the worst drawdown, the peak is the highest portfolio value before the trough, the recovery date is the first month after the trough when the value reaches that peak again, and “not yet recovered” means it had not reached it by the end of the period.

Data: month-end adjusted closes (including reinvested dividends), see data.js. Past performance does not predict future results; taxes, trading costs, and inflation are not included.

Why QQQM and SGOV have shorter histories: QQQM launched in October 2020 and SGOV in May 2020, so the common starting point for all thirteen ETFs is October 2020. “All” therefore covers about 6 years, not decades. A longer history, using proxy indices or older ETFs, is a possible next step.

Data source: see META in data.js.
